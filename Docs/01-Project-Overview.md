Project Overview

Background

Recycle4Change (R4C) is a Victorian social enterprise focused on building a sustainable recycling ecosystem through container collection services, environmental initiatives, and community engagement programs.

The organisation collaborates with industry partners and government stakeholders to improve recycling accessibility, reduce landfill waste, and create positive social outcomes through employment opportunities and environmental impact programs.

⸻

Business Problem

Recycle4Change required a digital platform capable of supporting end-to-end recycling operations while maintaining efficiency, scalability, and a positive customer experience.

Key business challenges included:

* Managing customer registration and profiles
* Tracking bins and recycling containers
* Managing truck fleet operations and maintenance
* Coordinating collection schedules
* Processing customer billing and payments
* Supporting charitable donations
* Measuring and reporting environmental impact

Without a structured system, operational visibility, customer communication, reporting capability, and process efficiency would become increasingly difficult as the organisation grows.

⸻

Project Objectives

The project aimed to:

1. Improve operational efficiency across recycling activities.
2. Increase customer engagement and service transparency.
3. Support effective fleet and collection management.
4. Enable accurate billing and donation processing.
5. Measure environmental impact through reporting and analytics.
6. Establish a scalable platform for future business growth.
