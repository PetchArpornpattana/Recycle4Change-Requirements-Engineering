# Stakeholder Analysis

## Business Context

Recycle4Change (R4C) operates within a complex ecosystem involving government agencies, recycling partners, customers, community groups, and internal operational teams.

To support the successful delivery of the proposed recycling management platform, stakeholder analysis was conducted to identify stakeholder needs, influence levels, communication requirements, and potential project impacts.

Understanding stakeholder expectations was critical to ensuring that business requirements aligned with organisational goals, operational processes, and user needs.

---

## Objective

The objectives of this stakeholder analysis were to:

- Identify key stakeholders involved in the project
- Understand stakeholder goals, concerns, and expectations
- Assess stakeholder influence and interest levels
- Develop appropriate stakeholder engagement strategies
- Support effective requirements elicitation and decision-making

---

## My Contribution

As the Business Analyst, I was responsible for:

- Identifying project stakeholders
- Analysing stakeholder interests and expectations
- Categorising stakeholders using a Power / Interest Grid
- Supporting stakeholder communication planning
- Aligning stakeholder needs with business requirements
- Contributing to requirements prioritisation activities

## Stakeholder Prioritisation

### High Power / High Interest

Stakeholders

- R4C Management Team
- TOMRA Cleanaway Victoria
- Victorian Government

Engagement Strategy

These stakeholders required close management through workshops, requirement reviews, project updates, and regular consultation sessions because they significantly influenced project outcomes and strategic direction.

---

### Low Power / High Interest

Stakeholders

- Residential Customers
- Commercial Customers
- Local Communities
- Collection Staff
- Customer Service Team

Engagement Strategy

These stakeholders required ongoing engagement through interviews, surveys, feedback sessions, and validation activities to ensure their needs were understood and incorporated into the proposed solution.

---

### High Power / Low Interest

Stakeholders

- Technology Providers
- Strategic Partners

Engagement Strategy

These stakeholders were kept satisfied through milestone updates, solution reviews, and decision-point communications to ensure continued support throughout the project lifecycle.

---

## Stakeholder Analysis Approach

A Power / Interest Grid was used to evaluate stakeholder influence and project interest levels.

This approach helped prioritise engagement efforts, improve communication planning, and ensure that stakeholder expectations were appropriately managed throughout the requirements engineering process.

---

## Business Value

Conducting stakeholder analysis provided several benefits:

- Improved understanding of stakeholder expectations
- Better alignment between business needs and project objectives
- More effective requirements elicitation activities
- Reduced risk of requirement conflicts and misunderstandings
- Enhanced stakeholder engagement and collaboration

---

## Key Outcome

The stakeholder analysis provided a structured understanding of stakeholder influence, project expectations, and communication needs.

The findings directly supported requirements elicitation, prioritisation activities, workshop planning, solution design decisions, and Agile backlog development throughout the Recycle4Change project.
