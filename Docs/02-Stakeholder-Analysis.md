Stakeholder Analysis

Purpose

Stakeholder analysis was conducted to identify key individuals, organisations, and groups that could influence or be affected by the Recycle4Change platform.

Understanding stakeholder needs, influence, and expectations was essential to ensure project success and alignment with business objectives.

⸻

Stakeholder Identification

The following stakeholder groups were identified during the analysis process:

| Stakeholder | Type |
|-------------|------|
| R4C Management Team | Internal |
| R4C Employees | Internal |
| TOMRA Cleanaway Victoria (TCV) | External |
| Fruit 2 Work (F2W) | External |
| Victorian Government | External |
| Local Community | External |
| Individual Customers | External |
| Commercial Customers | External |
| Residential Customers | External |
| Technology Providers | External |
| Donor Organisations | External |

⸻

Stakeholder Analysis Approach

A Power / Interest Grid was used to categorise stakeholders according to:

* Level of influence over the project
* Level of interest in project outcomes

This approach helped prioritise communication and engagement activities throughout the project lifecycle.

⸻

Key Stakeholders

High Power / High Interest

* R4C Management Team
* TOMRA Cleanaway Victoria
* Victorian Government

These stakeholders required close management and regular communication because they significantly influenced project outcomes.

Low Power / High Interest

* R4C Employees
* Local Community
* Individual Customers
* Commercial Customers
* Residential Customers

These stakeholders required ongoing engagement and communication to ensure their needs were understood and addressed.

High Power / Low Interest

* Technology Providers
* Donor Organisations

These stakeholders required periodic updates and satisfaction management due to their influence on project resources and infrastructure.

⸻

Stakeholder Engagement Strategy

Key engagement activities included:

* Stakeholder interviews
* Workshops
* Surveys and questionnaires
* Requirements validation sessions
* Regular project communication

These activities supported effective requirements gathering and stakeholder alignment throughout the project.

⸻

Outcome

The stakeholder analysis provided a clear understanding of stakeholder priorities, influence, and expectations.

This information was used to support requirements elicitation, prioritisation, communication planning, and solution design decisions.
