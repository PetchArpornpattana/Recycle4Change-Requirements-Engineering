Recycle4Change Requirements Engineering Project

Project Overview

Recycle4Change (R4C) is a recycling management platform designed to improve container collection operations, customer engagement, fleet management, billing, donations, and environmental impact tracking.

This project demonstrates the application of Business Analysis and Requirements Engineering techniques to transform business needs into structured requirements, process models, user stories, acceptance criteria, and Agile delivery artefacts.

⸻

Business Challenge

Recycle4Change needed a scalable platform to support:

* Customer registration and management
* Bin assignment and tracking
* Truck fleet management
* Collection scheduling
* Billing and payments
* Charity donations
* Environmental impact measurement

The challenge was to define clear business requirements while balancing operational efficiency, customer experience, and sustainability goals.
